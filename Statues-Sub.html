            Statues: start and stop points

            Teacher only. Pick a song, then tap or type where each part starts and stops. The game plays each part, stops the music at its stop point and shows a statue.

            0:00.0
            Space tap a start, then tap a stop. Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo. P play or pause. ← → back or forward 5 seconds. Click the waveform to jump there. Drag a part to move it, or drag its red edge to change where it starts or stops.
